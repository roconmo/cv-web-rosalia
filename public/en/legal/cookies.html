Cookie policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser to remember information about your visit. Some are needed for the website to work and others are used, for example, to measure how many people visit it.

2. Cookies used on this website

Name Owner Purpose Duration Type
rous-cookie-consent This website Remembers whether you accepted or rejected analytics cookies. Stored in your browser's local storage. Until you delete it Technical, necessary
_ga Google (Google Analytics) Distinguishes visitors anonymously to produce usage statistics. 2 years Analytics, third party. Only if you accept.
_ga_BF1MPYX4HK Google (Google Analytics) Keeps the session state for the statistics. 2 years Analytics, third party. Only if you accept.

Analytics cookies are not installed until you click "Accept" in the cookie notice. If you reject them, the website works exactly the same.

3. Other external services

  • Google Fonts serves the website's fonts. It does not set cookies, but it receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Forminit handles the contact form when you send it.
  • Calendly opens on its own website when you click "Book a call". Its cookies are governed by Calendly's policy, not this website's.
  • WhatsApp is only involved if you click the button to message me there.
