Radar: market intelligence in your inbox every morning

Every day an n8n workflow collects the sector's news, AI analyses it for one specific company and management receives a report that takes a few minutes to read.

  • Recommendation of the day: one concrete action for today.
  • Signals and moves from competitors, distributors and manufacturers.
  • Opportunities and risks for the business, ranked.
  • A suggested LinkedIn post based on the day's news.

I currently run it for a bathroom and ceramics distributor. The example is real, with identifying details removed.

Discover Radar3Min

From: Radar (n8n)

Subject: Radar · 27/10/2026

Recommendation of the day

Contact the 3 to 5 most active architecture and interior design studios in the area today and invite them to a private showroom visit in November, using the sensory design trends from Hotel Hábitat 2026 and Kaldewei's award-winning project at Casa Decor 2026 as a hook. Goal: strengthen the company's position as the local reference for specifiers before Roca and Laufen's showroom strategy gains more traction.

Most relevant signal

Roca opens the Zagreb Gallery with its premium brand Laufen, reinforcing its bet on experiential spaces for architects and designers, an area the company should also develop through its own events or its showroom.

See the full report

Early signals

Sensory design is gaining real traction: Hotel Hábitat 2026 and Kaldewei suggest that the tactile, visual and emotional experience of the bathroom will become an increasingly strong selling point, especially in premium residential and hotel projects during 2027. Robotics in ceramics manufacturing points to shorter product cycles and more customisation.

Market moves

Large brand showrooms are courting architects as a specification channel, which may reduce the distributor's role. Salvador Escoda continues its territorial expansion with a high-volume model. Prissmacer and Sacmi Ibérica show a Spanish ceramics sector investing in technology.

Opportunities

  1. An event for architects and interior designers at the showroom in Q4 2026 or Q1 2027.
  2. Explore sensory design bathroom materials with the main supplier for the hotel and premium segments.
  3. Extend the offer towards bathrooms integrated with heating and thermal comfort.

Risks

  1. Direct specification by manufacturers may weaken the distributor's role with design studios.
  2. The expansion of large general distributors may put pressure on market share among installers.
  3. Faster manufacturing may speed up stock obsolescence.

Suggested LinkedIn post

Bathrooms are no longer designed with the eyes alone. ✋ Kaldewei takes its project "Fuerza Suave", awarded at Casa Decor 2026, to Berlin, putting sensory design at the heart of the bathroom experience… Have you started using sensory criteria when choosing materials for a bathroom project?
